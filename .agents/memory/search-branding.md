name: Search branding
description: Search-engine site names and favicon requirements for the EA Migrate Pro web artifact.
---

Google search results can show the hosting provider as the site label when the site does not provide strong identity signals. Keep `application-name`, `og:site_name`, explicit Open Graph/Twitter identity, and a square transparent favicon aligned to the product name.

**Why:** The result previously showed “Vercel” and a generic icon even though the page title already said EA Migrate Pro; a tall logo alone was not a reliable search favicon.

**How to apply:** For future branding changes, update the root metadata, route-level social metadata, manifest icons, and favicon together. Expect Google to require a recrawl before the visible result changes.
